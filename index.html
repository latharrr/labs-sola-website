Questions, answered.

Is Sola a real bank?

No. Sola is a fictional banking concept designed by Deepanshu Labs to show how a bank for freelancers could feel. It is not a bank and no accounts are opened.

What happens when I enter my email?

You get one email about this concept and how it was made. Your address is shared with Deepanshu so he can reply. Nothing else is sent.

How would the money get split?

You pick a tax rate and a savings slice once. Every payment is then split into Tax, Savings and Spend the moment it lands, and the card only spends from Spend.

Can I get a website like this?

Yes. Deepanshu Labs builds websites and AI products. Say hello on WhatsApp or by email.
