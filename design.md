# Sola: design system (copied from mercury.com, captured 2026-10-29)

Sola is a fictional bank for freelancers, made for a demo. Story: you get paid, Sola splits it into Tax, Savings and Spend on its own.

## Section order (Mercury's order, Sola's content)
1. **Hero** (Mercury: misty ridge photo). Level 1: the Sola mark in 3D, full bleed. Centred H1, one line, email field + accent pill + ghost pill.
2. **Built for strip** (Mercury: "Loved by 450K+"). A slow marquee of who it is for, words not logos.
3. **Statement** (Mercury: "Banking's been a headache"). One sentence, word-light on scroll.
4. **Film** (Mercury: laptop push-in). Level 2 scroll film, framed right, captions left.
5. **How it works** (Mercury: "Everything you do with money" split list). List left, Level 3 living pipeline on a white dot-grid island right.
6. **Three cards** (Mercury: "Standard protection stops short"). Level 2 stills, hover lift + zoom.
7. **Close** (Mercury: "Banking redesigned from the ground up"). Eclipse CTA, one line, one button.
8. **Footer** (Mercury: link columns + disclaimers). Columns + "Sola is a fictional brand made for a demo."

Sections fade into each other with tall gradients (dark to warm cream and back). No hard edges.
